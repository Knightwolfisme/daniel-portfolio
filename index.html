I am a developer and robotics engineer specializing in the intersection of autonomous navigation, real-time computer vision, and high-performance systems software with a dash of exploratory fun.

From reconstructing 3D environments shot on drone hardware during 12-hour high-pressure hackathons to preparing for ISRO's Inter-collegiate space robotics challenge (IRoC-U 2026), my focus is on building resilient systems that perform under harsh constraints.

When I am not tuning PID controllers or analyzing camera point clouds, I build native desktop utilities like OneAtATime that streamline process scheduling and Games like "Do Not Play This Game(under development) to fill in the gaps between projects.
